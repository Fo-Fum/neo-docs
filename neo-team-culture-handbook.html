Neo Café Eatery Section One · Our Journey
Where Neo Came From

Neo Café has roots. Founded in 1993 by Andrew Economous, we opened our doors at a time when Wellington's café scene was just finding its feet. The name was inspired by the traditional Greek Kafeneio — a gathering place for community, conversation, and good coffee. That spirit has never left us.

Over the years, through a few changes in ownership, we're still here. A little older, a good deal wiser, and still committed to serving some of the city's best food and coffee. We've worked hard to build trust with our customers and a reputation we're proud of — but what matters more to us is what our regulars feel when they walk through the door.

Hospitality is in our DNA — and we're asking you to help us protect and grow it.

Why We're Here — Our Mission
Our Mission
To create special moments and build community — through great food, intentional service, and generous hospitality.

In a world that's more connected and more disconnected than ever, the table is one of the last places people still come together. That's the thing we're really in the business of — not just food and coffee, but creating an experience that delights and unites, with connection and belonging at its heart. A place people can trust and know they will be taken care of.

When someone walks out a little lighter than they walked in — when they leave with a memory worth sharing — that's the job done.

Get that right, and we're far more than a café.

Looking After Yourself

Hospitality is demanding — physically, mentally, and emotionally. We want you to genuinely thrive here, not just survive your shifts. That means looking after yourself like it matters, because it does.

Stay Hydrated

This one's easy to forget when you're busy and in your flow — so build it into your routine. Keep water within reach. Actually drink it. Your energy, your mood, and the quality of your work will all thank you.

Eat and Take Your Breaks

Eat something before you arrive. Take your breaks properly — they're yours, and you've earned them. Running on caffeine alone isn't a badge of honour; it's a fast track to burning out.

When Things Get Overwhelming

Busy services can get intense. When they do, a genuine pause — even just a few slow breaths — can reset your focus more effectively than pushing through on adrenaline. If you're really struggling, tell your manager. We work as a team and someone will step in without drama or judgement.

Notice Your Teammates

Looking after yourself includes noticing when someone else needs it. A simple "you doing okay?" at the right moment can genuinely shift someone's day. We're a crew — and that's what crews do for each other.

Remember
A thriving team is what makes a thriving café. Take care of yourself so you can take care of everyone else.

Our Standards

We want Neo to be a place where every person — team member, guest, or visitor — feels respected, safe, and valued. That doesn't happen by accident. It requires all of us to hold a clear standard.

If something's off — whether it's behaviour, service quality, or a safety concern — address it. Not with gossip, and not with drama. Address it directly, professionally, and with the intention of making things better, not worse.

Zero Tolerance

Neo is a place where everyone deserves to feel safe and respected. We have absolutely no tolerance for harassment, discrimination, or bullying of any kind.

Sexual Harassment

Unwanted comments, gestures, or behaviour of a sexual nature — regardless of intent.

Bullying

Repeated, harmful, or humiliating behaviour directed at any individual.

Discrimination

Treating someone unfairly based on their race, gender, sexuality, religion, age, disability, or any other protected characteristic.

Abusive Customers

We value inclusivity, and we're happy to swallow our pride to let a customer save face if needed — however, not every customer has to be our customer. We always ask that you respect everyone who walks through our door, though that respect flows both ways. If a customer is abusive, threatening, or harassing toward the team, it is our right to refuse service or ask someone to leave. If an incident occurs, report it immediately to a manager. The path forward is always de-escalation, not a showdown of egos.

Speak up
Letting harmful behaviour go unreported is not loyalty to the team — it is letting the team down.

Neo Café Eatery Section Five · Perks, Pay & Practicalities
The Perks of Working at Neo

Working at Neo comes with real benefits. Here's what's included — and the expectations that come with them.

Staff Discount

50% off menu items and cold beverages while you're on shift or on days off. Settle your tab weekly. If it exceeds $150 or goes unpaid, your discount is paused and you must pay as you go, until it's cleared.

Food Perks

Staff bread offcuts and spreads when available — check with the kitchen. One scone (at a manager's discretion) and one banana per shift, enjoyed during your break. Not taken home.

Hot Drinks

Up to three free hot drinks per shift (iced coffee included). Six Barrel sodas at $1.50. Sparkling water and leftover filter coffee are free.

On Your Days Off

We offer one complimentary hot drink per day. After that, the 50% staff discount applies. If you visit when we're busy, customers will take priority on service — unless you're visiting with other guests, in which case you'll receive our standard service.

Leftover Food

Any food that's unsold or unfit for service may be shared among the team at a manager's discretion.

Important
Staff discounts are for current team members only — not for friends, family, or former staff. Sharing your perks is an offence of serious misconduct.

Annual Leave, Sick Leave & Rosters
Annual Leave
Leave begins accruing from your first day of work.
You're entitled to four weeks per year, calculated on your average weekly hours.
You can only take paid leave if you have sufficient accrual — otherwise leave will be unpaid.
Request leave in writing via the appropriate Slack channel and verbally to your manager.
Leave when you want it is not guaranteed. It's approved based on business needs and available cover; however, we do our very best to accommodate requests.
Important
Do not book flights or make plans until your leave is formally approved. The more notice you give, the more likely we can make it work.
Sick, Bereavement & Emergency Leave
Call your manager as soon as you know you can't make a shift — call first; if you can't get through, then text. If you don't get a response, assume the message hasn't made it through: keep trying, or ring someone else on the team.
If you are sick, stay in communication with your manager with updates on when you're fit to return — this is your responsibility.
Record the leave on your timesheet, or ask a manager to do it for you, and get your manager's approval for it to be paid.
Rosters
Most rosters are fairly consistent, but flexibility is required to cover gaps. Part of being a team means we cover for each other, because what goes around comes around. It's up to all of us to cover gaps where we can — this keeps the business running smoothly and ensures everyone can take annual leave and sick leave as needed. And there will be times when cover is needed between hiring shortfalls.
Rosters are confirmed the week prior and posted just before the commencing week, allowing enough flexibility for any last-minute changes.
Notify your manager of any unavailability as early as possible — last-minute requests can't always be accommodated. The more notice you give, the more likely we can make it work.

Neo Café Eatery Section Six · Growth & Contribution
Menu Development
New Menu Ideas, Chef Specials & Barista Specials

Our menu is a living thing — it evolves to reflect the seasons, our customers, and the creativity of the people who work here. If you have an idea for a dish that could belong on it, we want to hear it.

1
Develop the Concept
What's the vision — and why does it belong on Neo's menu? Is it seasonal, trend-led, or nostalgic? What's the hero ingredient, and how do the flavours, textures, and colours work together? Is the produce readily available and reasonably priced? Is the dish practical to prep and serve?
2
Pitch It
Share the concept with your manager or head chef for initial feedback and approval before going further.
3
Cost It Out
Contact suppliers for current pricing. Factor in seasonality and potential price variation. Target a food cost of 27–32% — speak to a manager if you need help with the calculation.
4
Trial It
Order ingredients and prepare the dish for the team to taste. Gather honest feedback and refine as needed. Post details in the #menuinfo Slack channel — name, price, photo, and dietary flags.
5
Lock It In
Present the dish to the team for tasting and training. Organise when it will be rolled out and announce it on Slack. A new dish on the menu is a real achievement — take a moment to acknowledge it.
Craft
We build the menu with creativity and with discipline. Passion combined with practicality is what makes a dish last.
